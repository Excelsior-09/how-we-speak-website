What is this guide?

Not a book. A swipe file you actually use.

Most relationship books give you principles to think about. The Script Collection gives you exact language to use — today, this week, the next time things get hard.

Each script includes the situation, the goal, the suggested wording, why it works, what tone to use, common mistakes to avoid, and follow-up language if your partner pushes back.

Example — Starting a Difficult Conversation
"There's something I've been wanting to talk about, and I want us to handle it well. Is now a good time, or should we find a moment when we're both ready?"
Why it works: You're flagging the topic without ambushing them. You're giving them agency over timing, which reduces defensiveness before the conversation even starts.
Example — Apologizing Sincerely
"I was wrong. I see how what I said hurt you — and I'm genuinely sorry. Not because I want us to move on, but because you deserved better than that."
Why it works: Acknowledges the specific harm. Separates the apology from a desire to end discomfort quickly. That difference is what makes it feel real.
Example — Setting a Boundary
"I love you, and I can't accept [specific behavior] anymore. I'm not saying this to hurt you — I'm saying this because I want us to work."
Why it works: Leads with love, states the limit clearly, removes blame from the statement. It's a declaration, not an attack.
