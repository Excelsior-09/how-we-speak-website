Exercises included

Every chapter ends with something to do, not just think about.

The Sex Talk Framework isn't a theory guide. It's a practice guide. Each chapter includes a reflection exercise, a conversation prompt, and an action step you can take this week.

Reflection Exercise
Your Desire Map
Each partner completes privately: what they enjoy, what they want to explore, what is a definite boundary. Then you compare — without pressure, with curiosity.
Conversation Prompt
The Check-In
"What's one thing about our intimacy you've been wanting to tell me but weren't sure how?" — Use this once a month, in a relaxed moment, not in bed.
Action Step
Initiation Practice
This week, initiate a non-sexual moment of physical connection — and notice what happens to the emotional temperature between you.
